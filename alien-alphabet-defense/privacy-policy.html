Alien Alphabet Defense – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 September 2026

In short: Alien Alphabet Defense does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no chat, no ads, no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to the internet. Game progress stays on your device.

This policy explains how the Alien Alphabet Defense app ("the app"), made by Hardik Bhatt ("we", "us"), handles information. The app is a learning game designed for children, and we take children's privacy seriously.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for, collect, or transmit any personal information, such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, location, contacts, or device identifiers (including the Android Advertising ID). The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send information anywhere.

Information stored on your device

To remember progress, the app saves a small file on the device containing:

  • stars earned on each level and the number of in-game coins,
  • which tank colours, shapes and missiles have been unlocked with in-game coins,
  • settings such as music, sound effects and spoken letters on/off.

This file never leaves the device and is deleted when the app is uninstalled. It can also be erased at any time with Parent Zone → Reset progress. In-game coins cannot be bought with real money.

Spoken letters (text-to-speech)

When "Speak letters aloud" is on, the app asks the device's built-in text-to-speech service to say the letter, number or word on screen. Only that game text is passed to the device's own speech service; we do not receive any audio or data. The app does not use the microphone.

Advertising and purchases

This version of the app contains no advertising and no in-app purchases. If a future version adds them, we will update this policy before that version is released. Any ads would come only from advertising networks certified for Google Play's Families program, configured for child-directed, non-personalised ads, and any purchase would be placed behind the grown-ups-only Parent Zone and processed by Google Play.

Children's privacy

The app is directed to children and is designed to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, and Google Play's Families Policy. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, including children, there is no personal information to access, correct or delete. Settings and purchases meant for adults are behind a Parent Zone that asks a grown-up question first.

Third-party services

The app does not include analytics, advertising, social media or other third-party data-collection software. Google Play, which distributes the app, has its own privacy policy for your Google account.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version at this address and change the effective date above. Significant changes, such as adding advertising, will be made before the app version that needs them is released.
