Alien Alphabet Defense – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

In short: Alien Alphabet Defense has no ads, no accounts and no chat. We (the developer) do not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information, and game progress stays on your device. A grown-up can buy the Full Game through Google Play from the Parent Zone.

This policy explains how the Alien Alphabet Defense app ("the app"), made by Hardik Bhatt ("we", "us"), handles information. The app is a learning game designed for children, and we take children's privacy seriously.

Information we collect

None. We do not ask for, collect, or receive any personal information, such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, location, contacts, or device identifiers (including the Android Advertising ID). We do not run any servers that receive data from the app. The app goes online only through Google Play's billing service, to show the Full Game price and process a purchase.

Information stored on your device

To remember progress, the app saves a small file on the device containing:

  • stars earned on each level and the number of in-game coins,
  • which tank colours, shapes and missiles have been unlocked with in-game coins,
  • whether the Full Game has been bought,
  • your child's first name, if a grown-up types it in (used only to show it in the game, for example in the Spell My Name world and in messages like "Great job, Aarav!"),
  • settings such as music, sound effects and spoken letters on/off.

This file never leaves the device and is deleted when the app is uninstalled. Progress can be erased at any time with Parent Zone → Reset progress, and the name can be changed in the Parent Zone. The name is never sent to us or to anyone else. In-game coins cannot be bought with real money.

Spoken letters (text-to-speech)

When "Speak letters aloud" is on, the app asks the device's built-in text-to-speech service to say the letter, number or word on screen. Only that game text is passed to the device's own speech service; we do not receive any audio or data. The app does not use the microphone.

Advertising

The app contains no advertising of any kind.

Purchases

A grown-up can buy the Full Game, a one-time purchase that opens all 8 worlds and 80 levels. The purchase button is inside the Parent Zone, which asks a grown-up question first. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; we never see or store card, bank or payment details, and we receive only the usual sales reports from Google Play that do not identify the child. Purchases can be restored on a new device with Parent Zone → Restore purchase while signed in to the same Google account.

Children's privacy

The app is directed to children and is designed to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, and Google Play's Families Policy. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, including children, there is no personal information held by us to access, correct or delete. Purchases and settings meant for adults are behind a Parent Zone that asks a grown-up question first.

Third-party services

  • Google Play Billing – processes the optional Full Game purchase.
  • Google Play – distributes the app and has its own privacy policy for your Google account.

The app does not include advertising, analytics, social media or other data-collection software.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version at this address and change the effective date above. Significant changes will be made before the app version that needs them is released.
