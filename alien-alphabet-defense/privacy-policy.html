Alien Alphabet Defense – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026

In short: We (the developer) do not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. There are no accounts and no chat, and game progress stays on your device. From version 1.1 the free version can show short, optional, kid-safe reward videos from Google AdMob, which are set up for children and never personalised. A grown-up can buy the Full Game through Google Play, which removes all ads.

This policy explains how the Alien Alphabet Defense app ("the app"), made by Hardik Bhatt ("we", "us"), handles information. The app is a learning game designed for children, and we take children's privacy seriously.

Information we collect

None. We do not ask for, collect, or receive any personal information, such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, location, or contacts. We do not run any servers that receive data from the app. The app does not request the Android Advertising ID.

Information stored on your device

To remember progress, the app saves a small file on the device containing:

  • stars earned on each level and the number of in-game coins,
  • which tank colours, shapes and missiles have been unlocked with in-game coins,
  • which levels have been opened by watching reward videos, and how many videos were watched today,
  • whether the Full Game has been bought,
  • settings such as music, sound effects and spoken letters on/off.

This file never leaves the device and is deleted when the app is uninstalled. It can also be erased at any time with Parent Zone → Reset progress. In-game coins cannot be bought with real money.

Spoken letters (text-to-speech)

When "Speak letters aloud" is on, the app asks the device's built-in text-to-speech service to say the letter, number or word on screen. Only that game text is passed to the device's own speech service; we do not receive any audio or data. The app does not use the microphone.

Advertising (free version)

The free version can show reward videos: short ads a child chooses to watch to double coins after a level, or to open a locked level (three videos per level). Ads never pop up on their own, and no more than 10 reward videos can be watched per day.

These ads are provided by Google AdMob, an advertising network certified for Google Play's Families program. Every ad request is marked as directed to children (COPPA "child-directed treatment") and as from a user under the age of consent, and is limited to ads rated G (suitable for general audiences). With these settings Google does not show personalised (interest-based) ads, does not build advertising profiles, and does not use remarketing. To deliver an ad and to prevent fraud, Google's ad software processes limited technical information such as the device's IP address, device and app information, and app-specific identifiers. This information goes directly to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. The app needs an internet connection only to load these videos and to talk to Google Play for purchases.

Purchases

A grown-up can buy Full Game + No Ads, a one-time purchase that opens all worlds and removes all ads. The purchase button is inside the Parent Zone, which asks a grown-up question first. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; we never see or store card, bank or payment details, and we receive only the usual sales reports from Google Play that do not identify the child. Purchases can be restored on a new device with Parent Zone → Restore purchase while signed in to the same Google account.

Children's privacy

The app is directed to children and is designed to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, and Google Play's Families Policy. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, including children, there is no personal information held by us to access, correct or delete. Purchases and settings meant for adults are behind a Parent Zone that asks a grown-up question first.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob – reward videos in the free version (child-directed, non-personalised, rated G), as described above.
  • Google Play Billing – processes the optional Full Game purchase.
  • Google Play – distributes the app and has its own privacy policy for your Google account.

The app does not include analytics, social media or other data-collection software.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version at this address and change the effective date above. Significant changes will be made before the app version that needs them is released.
